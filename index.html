Our Story

A Decade of Kings

01

Esports With a $10M Prize Pool

The international championship keeps leveling up year after year, with a ten-million-dollar prize pool pushing competitive 5V5 MOBA onto the world stage.

02

Leading the Mobile MOBA Genre

Launched in 2015 by TiMi Studio Group, a decade of hero redesigns, worldbuilding and gameplay tuning turned it into the mobile MOBA phenomenon it is today.

03

Played by Millions Every Day

With 100 million daily active players, the game grew far past a pastime — into a social space where creators and communities built whole careers around it.

04

Crafted With Global Partners

Soundtracks composed with world-renowned artists — Hans Zimmer, Joe Hisaishi, Howard Shore — earned the score a Global Music Awards gold medal.
